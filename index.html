Faro for your TV

A player for the IPTV subscription you already have. It has no channels of its own.

Download Faro for TV

Version 0.3.154 · November 26, 2026 · 90 MB · Google TV, Android TV and Fire TV

Before you start

  • A TV or stick with Google TV or Android TV, or a Fire TV from 2018 or later (Fire OS 6 or newer; tried on a Fire TV Stick 4K). For an iPhone or an Apple TV, ask the person who sent you this address: those are installed another way.
  • Your own IPTV subscription: the server address, the user name and the password your provider gave you. Faro comes with no subscription.

Install it on a Google TV or Android TV

  1. On the TV, open the Play Store and install Downloader (by AFTVnews).
  2. Open Downloader, type faro.bigintdata.com and choose Go. This page opens: choose Download.
  3. The first time, the TV asks whether Downloader may install apps. Choose Settings, turn it on for Downloader, and go back. Then choose Install, and Open.
  4. Sign in with the server address, user name and password from your provider.

Install it on a Fire TV

  1. On the Fire TV, go to Find, search for Downloader (by AFTVnews) and install it.
  2. Allow Downloader to install apps: Settings, My Fire TV, Developer options, Install unknown apps, and turn it on for Downloader. On an older Fire TV the switch is called Apps from Unknown Sources: turn that on.
  3. If there is no Developer options: Settings, My Fire TV, About, and press Select seven times on the first line (the Fire TV's name). Go back, and Developer options is there.
  4. Open Downloader, type faro.bigintdata.com and choose Go. This page opens: choose Download, then Install, and Open.
  5. Sign in with the server address, user name and password from your provider. The first time, the list of movies can take up to a minute to appear; after that it opens at once.

When a new version comes

Come back to this address and do the same again. The new version installs over the old one, and your sign-in and favorites stay.

The version you have is in the app, under Settings.

On a Fire TV, the app's picture in the list of apps can go blank after an update. Restart the Fire TV (Settings, My Fire TV, Restart) and it comes back.

If something goes wrong

Tell the person who sent you this address what you were doing when it happened, and the version shown in Settings.
