LibVLC (VLC for Android 3.6.5) and LibVLCSharp 3.10.2 in this app
===================================================================

This app plays video with LibVLC (VideoLAN; NuGet package VideoLAN.LibVLC.Android 3.6.5, built from the VLC for Android 3.6.5
sources) through LibVLCSharp 3.10.2 (VideoLAN). Both are licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or (at
your option) any later version. The full licence text is in LGPL-2.1.txt next to this file; the VLC authors are listed in
VLC-AUTHORS.txt.

Copyright (C) the VLC authors and VideoLAN (LibVLC); Copyright (C) VideoLAN and the LibVLCSharp authors (LibVLCSharp).

Source code
-----------
- LibVLC for Android: https://code.videolan.org/videolan/vlc-android/-/tree/3.6.5
- LibVLCSharp:        https://code.videolan.org/videolan/LibVLCSharp/-/tree/3.10.2
- The Android build:  https://code.videolan.org/videolan/libvlc-nuget

LibVLC is used as unmodified shared libraries (lib/<abi>/libvlc.so in the APK), which you may replace with your own build of the same
version under the terms of the LGPL.
